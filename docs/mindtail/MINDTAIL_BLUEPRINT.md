## 2. Content positioning

MINDTAIL is entertainment for scrolling audiences---not a classroom,
lecture, or breaking-news channel. Prioritize evergreen and replayable
content.

### Pillars

1.  **Dark psychology / mind & behaviour:** manipulation patterns,
    cognitive biases, persuasion, social dynamics. Explain responsibly;
    do not diagnose or claim a tactic works on everyone.
2.  **Human behaviour:** group behaviour, friendships, relationships,
    online spaces, pressure.
3.  **Relationships & social reality:** toxic friendship patterns,
    betrayal, trust, jealousy, boundaries. Avoid revenge/harassment or
    blanket distrust.
4.  **Mystery & suspense:** unusual mysteries, strange incidents,
    historical oddities. Separate verified facts from speculation.
5.  **Real-life stories:** unusual/emotional stories with a reveal.
    Verify real stories; label fiction/dramatization clearly.
6.  **Social media & modern life:** attention, comparison, scrolling,
    online identity, digital behaviour.

### Avoid

Expired daily news, generic motivation, fake psychology facts,
fabricated statistics/studies, unverified conspiracies presented as
truth, exploitative tragedy, graphic shock, and copied content without
original transformation.

## 3. Short storytelling formula

Use flexibly: 1. **Hook (0--2 sec):** curiosity gap, contradiction,
question, or surprising statement. 2. **Setup (2--10 sec):** context. 3.
**Escalation (10--35 sec):** add detail/conflict/stakes frequently. 4.
**Payoff (last 10--20 sec):** reveal, twist, insight, or satisfying
conclusion. 5. **Optional ending:** natural comment prompt; no begging
or fake cliffhangers.

Retention rules: one main idea per Short; no greeting/logo intro;
narration starts immediately; short spoken-language sentences; every
line advances the story; visuals change with story beats; deliver the
hook's promise. Target **45--90 seconds**, but test shorter lengths and
never pad.

## 4. Language & narration

-   Conversational Hindi; natural Hinglish where appropriate.
-   Expressive, controlled, suspenseful voice---not robotic or
    exaggerated horror.
-   AI narration (creator has experience with ElevenLabs) is acceptable
    only under provider terms and plan rights.
-   Review pronunciation, pacing, pauses, and emphasis.
-   Never imitate a real person without permission.

### Script checklist

-   Strong first line?
-   Clear without prior context?
-   Claims supportable?
-   Hook paid off?
-   Natural aloud?
-   Original and sufficiently transformed?
-   Ending satisfying?

## 8. End-to-end production workflow

Creator approval is required for factual scripts, assets, final render,
and public publishing by default.

### A. Ideation

Generate batches across pillars. Score curiosity, evergreen potential,
emotional/suspense value, visual feasibility, originality, and evidence
availability. Reject misinformation or harmful stereotypes. Save
selected ideas to backlog.

### B. Research

For factual/real stories, prefer primary, reputable, academic, or
official sources. Record URL, date, and exact claim supported. Separate
facts, interpretations, disputes, and unknowns. Never invent citations.
Label fiction clearly.

### C. Script

Create several hook options, then concise Hindi script with beat timing,
visual suggestions, sound cues, and source notes. Fact-check,
originality-check, policy-check, and review natural Hindi. Creator
approves before voice production.

### D. Voice

Generate only after script approval. Use authorized API access and
comply with plans/terms. Do not rotate accounts to evade quotas. Listen
fully and correct pronunciation/pacing. Store audio and permitted
model/settings metadata.

### E. Visuals

Build shot list mapped to narration. Generate/source rights-safe
visuals. Maintain continuity. Avoid impersonation, misleading
depictions, and unauthorized copyrighted footage. Store prompts and
provenance.

### F. Edit

Assemble visuals, narration, captions, music, and effects. Maintain 9:16
and safe text margins. Check hook, caption accuracy, narration clarity,
and audio mix. Watch full render before approval.

### G. Package

Prepare 3--5 titles, description, relevant hashtags, cover frame if
useful, credits/source notes, metadata, and synthetic-content disclosure
decision.

### H. Publish

Creator reviews final video and metadata. Upload private/unlisted for
review if desired. Publish/schedule only after explicit approval. Record
URL, date, title, and asset references.

### I. Analyze

Review at 24h/7d/28d where data exists: views/velocity, viewed vs swiped
away, average view duration/percentage, retention/drop-offs,
likes/comments/shares/subscribers, returning viewers, and comparisons by
hook/topic/length. Don't overreact to one video; improve clarity and
payoff rather than clickbait.

## 9. Initial cadence & 30-video test

Start at **3--5 Shorts/week** if quality is sustainable; batch work and
keep a backlog. For first 30 videos, test: - 5 human behaviour - 5
relationship/social reality - 5 psychology/cognitive-bias - 5
mysteries - 5 unusual real-life stories - 5 social media/modern life

Record pillar, hook, duration, format, visual style, title style, and
metrics. Use results to shape next batch; no growth guarantees.

## 10. Responsible content & policy

-   Follow YouTube Community Guidelines, copyright, privacy, and
    monetization policies.
-   Use original scripts and meaningful creative value.
-   Obtain rights/licenses for footage, music, images, and fonts.
-   Do not portray AI scenes as genuine evidence.
-   Disclose realistic altered/synthetic content when YouTube requires
    it.
-   No fake medical/psychological diagnoses or advice.
-   No accusations against identifiable private people.
-   No graphic/exploitative content, fabricated
    studies/statistics/quotes/sources, or misleading speculation.
-   Label fiction, reenactments, and speculation when viewers could be
    misled.
-   Avoid mass-produced/repetitive minimally transformed uploads.
-   No purchased views, spam, deceptive metadata, or engagement
    manipulation.

## 12. Claude Code implementation roadmap

### Phase 1 --- Foundation

Create workspace, README, brand guidelines,
idea/script/research/metadata/QC templates, and backlog. Preserve
existing files.

### Phase 2 --- Script workspace

Input idea → hook options → Hindi script → duration estimate → source
notes → quality/policy checks → approval-ready output.

### Phase 3 --- Research provenance

Store source URL, claim, evidence, confidence, and date checked. Flag
unsupported claims.

### Phase 4 --- Audio/visual workflow

Integrate TTS only through authorized documented APIs. Keep secrets in
environment variables/secret manager; never commit `.env`. Create
prompts, shot lists, audio review, and caption workflow. Check current
official docs; don't assume APIs/quotas.

### Phase 5 --- Editing/export

Use available tools or compatible project assets. Standardize
resolution, frame rate, audio, subtitles, safe margins. Validate
dimensions, duration, audio, and file size. Keep editable project and
final render.

### Phase 6 --- Publishing

Prepare review package. If YouTube API is considered, check official
docs, OAuth scopes, quotas, and policies. Publishing remains
approval-gated unless explicitly authorized.

### Phase 7 --- Analytics

Import Studio exports or authorized API data. Build tracker/dashboard
and evidence-based recommendations.
