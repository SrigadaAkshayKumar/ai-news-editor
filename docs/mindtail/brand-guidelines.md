# MINDTAIL brand guidelines

**Channel:** MINDTAIL · @MIND_TAIL_99 · India · Hindi/Hinglish · Shorts only
**Tagline:** none (dropped 2026-11-10)

## What MINDTAIL is
Fast, gripping Hindi Shorts for young Indian viewers: facts that surprise,
how the mind works, stories and mysteries, motivation and life lessons, modern
life and social media, and relatable youth moments. Built from stock B-roll,
bold animated text and punchy sound design.

**Personality:** curious, sharp, cinematic, a little intense. Shocking because
it's true, not because it's made up.

## Content rules
- "Shocking" must still be **accurate**: source every factual claim (`research.md`).
- No fake statistics, studies, quotes or "scientists say" without a source.
- No medical or psychological diagnoses or advice.
- No gore, exploitative tragedy, or accusations against identifiable private people.
- Label fiction and dramatisations on screen.
- Stock footage of real people must not imply something false about them.

## Visual identity
| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Canvas | 1080×1920, 30 fps |
| Palette | black, white/silver, **electric cyan `#00E5FF`** (brand accent) |
| Accents | yellow `#FFD700` and red `#FF303B` sparingly, for warnings/shock words |
| Grade | the pipeline's `grade: true` look: higher contrast, slightly cool, soft vignette |
| Fonts | Noto Sans Devanagari Bold (Hindi), Noto Sans Bold (Latin), switched automatically |
| Text | big, few words per line, highlight 1-2 key words, white with black outline |
| Safe area | keep text out of the bottom fifth and the right edge (Shorts buttons) |
| Pace | a visual change every 1.5-3 s; no logo intro |
| Logo | emblem only, no text: silver brain-like swirl with a central blue eye |

## Text styles (pipeline names)
- `hook`: the first line and big reveals
- `caption`: normal lines
- `small`: secondary info, source notes
- `tag`: cyan label box ("PART 2", "FACT #3")

## Sound
- Music sets the mood; sound effects mark cuts (whoosh), reveals (riser → impact) and pops of text.
- Final loudness is about -14 LUFS with true peak at or below -1.5 dBTP (set automatically).
- With a voiceover, the voice always wins: music ducks under it.
- Only music and SFX whose licence permits YouTube use; record credits.

## Banner and logo
Banner: dark blue cinematic background with electric-blue light, MINDTAIL name
centred in the safe area, no tagline. At least 2048×1152 px, under 6 MB. Profile
logo: emblem only, no text, readable at small size.
