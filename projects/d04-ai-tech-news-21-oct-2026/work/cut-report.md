Salesforce has agreed to buy the marketing platform, Braze, for about 5.66 billion dollars, and Microsoft is building controls to watch and restrict the AI agents running inside companies. Let's get into it. First, OpenAI is reportedly in talks to raise at least 30 billion dollars. Reuters and Bloomberg report that the round would value the chat GPT maker at about 1.4 trillion dollars. Bloomberg says Abu Dhabi's MGX is discussing a group of funds from the United Arab Emirates worth up to 10 billion dollars, with BlackRock alongside. The talks are still going, and no lead investor has been named. OpenAI and BlackRock declined to comment. This is a report, not a closed deal, but it shows how much money the biggest AI labs still need to keep building. Next, Salesforce is buying Braze, a customer engagement platform used to send messages through apps, email, text, and WhatsApp. According to Salesforce's filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the deal values Braze at about 5.66 billion dollars. Salesforce will pay $47.50 in cash for each Braze share. The deal still needs approval from Braze shareholders and regulators. Salesforce expects it to close in its fiscal year 2028. For marketers, it means two big tools may soon become one. Third, Microsoft has announced new security controls for AI agents. In a blog post on October 19th, Microsoft said its tools can now discover 60 types of agents installed on employee computers. That list includes Cloud Code, OpenAI Codex, and GitHub Copilot. A new feature called Dynamic Agent Risk gives each agent a risk score that changes with its behavior. Another, Dynamic Permissions starts agents with no access and grants only what one task needs for a limited time. Microsoft has not given release dates. It points to its Ignite event in November. The message to companies is simple. Treat agents like insiders. Now to chips. NVIDIA has won Modal, a fast-growing cloud startup, as a customer for its new Vira processor. Reuters reports that Modal will use Vira for the first time to host AI agents. Until now, it ran that work only on Intel and advanced micro devices chips. Modal also said it raised 750 million dollars at a 15.75 billion dollar valuation. NVIDIA says it expects at least 20 billion dollars from Vira sales this fiscal year. Each customer like Modal helps NVIDIA move into the processor market. Amazon plans to spend more than 16 billion euros in Germany in 2027. That is about 18 billion dollars. The company announced it on the October 20th at the Invest in Germany Summit in Berlin. 4.8 billion euros is for expanding the Amazon Web Services region in Frankfurt. The rest covers warehouses, a second robotics and AI research lab in Berlin, and its satellite internet network. Amazon says it has invested more than 100 billion euros in Germany since 2010. Data centers are where the AI race is physically built. Now, a warning for anyone running a WordPress site. Security Week reports that attackers slipped malware into RankMath Pro, a popular search engine plugin. Version 3.0.123 was affected. Packages with the malware were available from the 15th of October to the 16th of October. If a site installed that version in that window and activated it, it should be treated as compromised. RankMath has removed the files and released a clean version, 3.0.124. Site owners should update, change passwords and keys, and restore from a clean backup if needed. Last, a patch worth applying today. F5 has released out of band fixes for the Engine X web server. Blee Ping Computer reports three critical flaws listed as CVE2026-15176-97205 and -93479. Remote attackers could use them to crash a server or, on some non-default setups, run code. No active attacks have been reported. Administrators should update to the latest version.
