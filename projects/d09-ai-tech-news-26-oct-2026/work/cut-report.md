Elon Musk said debt markets have almost stopped lending to AI companies, without large revenue elsewhere. A new study shows how differently two chatbots handle politics. Researchers at Convovo, led by Cornell professor Tapan Parikh, asked models through their developer interfaces to pick one candidate in nearly 500 mid-term races. Grok 4.7 chose the Republican in 92% of answers. OpenAI, GPT, 6.1 Soul chose the Democrat in 97%. The two agreed less than 10% of the time. The consumer apps for chat GPT and Grok declined to name a candidate. OpenAI says the test does not reflect typical use, space XAI did not respond. The standard H2 costs $29,900, the H2X has no price yet. It is also unclear whether the demos were autonomous or remote-controlled, treat the videos as marketing until the robot is tested.
